KoSpeaker is an offline Android Text-to-Speech engine (a fork of SherpaTTS) built to give KOReader on Onyx Boox e-ink devices a natural, neural voice for Read Aloud of EPUBs.

Install a voice once from the in-app Manage Languages screen — that download is the only time the app touches the network. After that, text handling, synthesis, and audio all run entirely on-device: no cloud, no account, no tracking.

Features: Piper neural voices (default), an optional Kokoro high-quality mode, offline Hebrew support, a prose-oriented reading pipeline (normalization, sentence-chunked streaming, number verbalization), and a guided "Set up with KOReader" onboarding screen.
